Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

Wavium ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Wavium mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (securely hashed). You may also provide your name for personalization.
  • User Content: Intentions and affirmations you create within the app are stored to provide the service.
  • Usage Data: We collect basic usage information such as streak data and session counts, stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Wavium service
  • To personalize your affirmations and experience
  • To generate AI-powered affirmation content based on your intentions
  • To authenticate your account

Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS). Audio content is stored on Cloudflare R2. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure authentication tokens.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database (Privacy Policy)
  • Groq: AI language model for generating affirmations (Privacy Policy)
  • Microsoft Edge TTS: Text-to-speech for audio generation
  • Cloudflare R2: Audio file storage

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app, which will permanently remove all your data from our servers.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your data within the app at any time.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from the home screen of the app.
  • Portability: Your affirmations and intentions are visible within the app.

Children's Privacy

Wavium is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
